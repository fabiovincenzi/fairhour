# Getting help

- 📚 **Documentation**: <https://fabiovincenzi.github.io/fairhour/>
- 💬 **Questions, ideas, show and tell**: [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/fabiovincenzi/fairhour/discussions).
  Search first; your question may already have an answer.
- 🐛 **Bugs**: open a [bug report](https://github.com/fabiovincenzi/fairhour/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)
  with steps to reproduce, your version and whether you self-host.
- ✨ **Feature ideas**: start a Discussion or open a
  [feature request](https://github.com/fabiovincenzi/fairhour/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- 🧮 **A tax pack for your country**: open a
  [tax pack request](https://github.com/fabiovincenzi/fairhour/issues/new?template=tax_pack_request.yml).
- 🔒 **Security issues**: never in public; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Fairhour is maintained by volunteers. There is no guaranteed response time and no commercial
support at the moment.

**Tax disclaimer**: Fairhour computes invoice figures to help you, but it is not tax or legal
advice. Always verify amounts and wording with a qualified accountant.
