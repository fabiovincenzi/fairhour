Time trackers tell you how many hours you worked. **Fairhour also tells you what to invoice.** A
pluggable, country-specific tax engine turns tracked time into the exact amounts on the invoice
(taxable base, contributions, VAT, withholding, stamp duty, total and net payable) and explains
every step with its legal source. Italy is the reference country; the community adds the others.

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Time tracking" icon="clock">
    A one-click timer that survives reloads, manual entries, quick add (`2h design Acme yesterday`),
    rounding per project and overlap detection. The web app is coming in v0.4.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tax engine with an explanation trace" icon="puzzle">
    Country packs compute every amount exactly and show each step with the law behind it. Italy
    (forfettario and ordinario) and a generic VAT pack come first, in v0.2.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reports" icon="analytics">
    Dashboards and charts by client, project, task and tag, PDF timesheets, CSV export and signed
    share links for clients. Coming in v0.5.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Desktop companion" icon="laptop">
    A tray timer, idle detection and private, opt-in activity suggestions that never leave your
    device. Coming in v0.9.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Public API" icon="external">
    A versioned REST API described by OpenAPI, personal access tokens and signed webhooks. Coming in
    v0.7.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Self-hosting first" icon="server">
    One `docker compose up`, PostgreSQL, documented environment variables and no telemetry unless
    you turn it on. Coming in v0.8.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="caution" title="Informational figures">
  Fairhour is in early development. Tax figures are **informational**: always verify them with your
  accountant before issuing an invoice. Fairhour does not provide tax or legal advice.
</Aside>
